# The Supporting Cast Has Unionized

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41`
("Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis", $2): the funniest single-line diagnosis
an AI therapist hands its human, 200 characters or fewer — 109 of 200.

> Diagnosis: chronic main-character syndrome — your supporting cast has unionized and filed a formal complaint.

## Why it should win

Built to the judge's brief, in the judge's own order. It *is* a diagnosis:
it opens with "Diagnosis:", names a syndrome, and stays in a therapist's
calm clinical voice all the way through. The turn lands at the very end —
"main-character syndrome" sets up a familiar self-centred habit, and the
punch is that the people around the patient have organised against it:
the supporting cast has unionized and filed a formal complaint. The laugh
arrives on the last clause, never before it. Every word earns its place
(109 characters, quotable in one breath), and the image is fresh — no open
tabs, no turning anything off and on again, no search history. A stranger
gets it in one read, and the line is funny enough to repeat out loud.

## Running it

`main.py` (Python 3.12, standard library only, no network) validates the
line against the 200-character gate and writes it byte for byte to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Two runs
write identical bytes.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py && cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
